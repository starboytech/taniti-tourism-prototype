Transportation

Getting to Taniti

Almost all visitors arrive in Taniti by air, though some arrive on a small cruise ship that docks in Yellow Leaf Bay one night per week. Taniti has a small airport that can accommodate small jets and propeller planes.

Ground Transportation

  • Public buses serve Taniti City and run every day from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m.
  • Private buses serve the rest of the island.
  • Taxis are available in Taniti City.
  • Rental cars are available near the airport.
  • Bikes and helmets are available to rent from several vendors (helmets are required by law).
  • Taniti City is fairly flat and very walkable, and the area around Merriton Landing, where many tourists stay, is easy to explore on foot.
