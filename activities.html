Activities

Entertainment

Most visitors come for the beaches, to explore the rainforest, and visit the volcano. Others come to visit the local history museum, go on chartered fishing tours, snorkeling, ziplining in the rainforest, helicopter rides, etc. Many of these activities are in Merriton Landing, which is on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay.

Sightseeing

Most tourists spend time in the city, which boasts native architecture and nearby white, sandy beaches that encircle Yellow Leaf Bay.

Holiday Closures

Taniti has many national holidays, and many tourist attractions are closed on holidays, so plan ahead.
