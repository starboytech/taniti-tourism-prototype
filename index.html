Welcome to Taniti Island

Taniti is a small tropical island in the Pacific. While the island has an area of less than 500 square miles, the terrain is varied and includes both sandy and rocky beaches, harbor, tropical rainforest, and a mountainous interior that includes a small active volcano. Taniti has an indigenous population of about 20,000. Until a recent increase in tourism, most of the economy was dominated by fishing and agriculture.
