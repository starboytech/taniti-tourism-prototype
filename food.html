Food

Restaurants

Taniti currently has 10 restaurants. They are mostly local ones and serve fish and rice. There are also a few that serve American-style meals, and a few others serving Pan-Asian cuisine.

Grocery Stores

Taniti has two supermarkets, two smaller grocery stores and one convenience store that's open 24/7.

Holiday Closures

Taniti has many national holidays, and many restaurants are closed on holidays, so plan ahead.
